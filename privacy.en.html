Privacy Policy

YOLO app

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use and protect your personal information when you use the YOLO app.

1. Information we collect

When you use the YOLO app we collect the following information:

1.1. Registration and authentication data

  • Phone number: used to register and sign in to the app via SMS verification
  • Email address: optional, used for account recovery and communication
  • Profile data: nickname, date of birth, gender, profile description, interests, Instagram link
  • Avatars: profile photos you upload

1.2. Location data

  • Current location: coordinates (latitude and longitude) for display on the map
  • Movement history: your location in the background for the "Bump" feature (meeting friends)
  • Moment location: location attached to the moments you publish
  • Address: a text description of your location (city, district)

Important: location data is collected only when you grant permission. You can turn off location access in your device settings at any time.

1.3. User-generated content

  • Moments: photos, videos and text messages you publish with a location
  • Events: events you create with a description, photos, date, venue and category
  • Stories: temporary content that disappears after a set time
  • Chat messages: text messages, photos, videos and voice messages in direct and group chats
  • Comments: comments on moments and events
  • Likes and reactions: information about which moments and events you like

1.4. Social interactions

  • Follows and followers: the list of users you follow and who follow you
  • Event participation: events you attend or have applied to
  • Invitations: events you've been invited to
  • Viewed stories and moments: information about viewed content for feed personalization

1.5. Usage data

  • Search history: saved search queries to improve results
  • App activity: time of last activity, frequency of feature use
  • App settings: selected theme color, notification settings, map visibility
  • Notifications: history of notifications received (new followers, likes, comments, messages)

1.6. Technical data

  • FCM token: a unique device identifier for sending push notifications
  • Device information: device model, operating system, app version
  • User ID: a unique ID assigned at registration
  • File metadata: information about uploaded media files (size, format, creation date)

2. Use of information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

2.1. Providing the app's core features

  • User registration and authentication via phone number or email
  • Creating and managing a user profile
  • Publishing moments with a location
  • Creating and joining events
  • Messaging in direct and group chats
  • Publishing stories
  • Searching for events, users and moments

2.2. Location features

  • Showing your location on the map (when visibility is on)
  • Showing events and moments in your area
  • The "Bump" feature — real-time notifications when you meet friends
  • Attaching moments to specific places
  • Personalizing content based on your location

2.3. Social features

  • Following other users and managing followers
  • A personalized feed of events and moments
  • Event recommendations based on your interests and location
  • Interacting with content (likes, comments, reposts)
  • Participating in events and managing requests

2.4. Notifications

  • Push notifications about new chat messages
  • Notifications about new followers
  • Notifications about likes and comments on your moments
  • Notifications about new events in your area
  • Notifications about event invitations
  • Notifications about meeting friends (Bump)

You can manage notification settings in the app.

2.5. Improving the service

  • Analyzing app usage to improve functionality
  • Fixing bugs and technical issues
  • Developing new features based on user behavior
  • Optimizing app performance

2.6. Security

  • Preventing fraud and abuse
  • Protecting users from harmful content
  • Ensuring the security of user data
  • Enforcing the app's terms of use

3. Data storage and processing

3.1. Storage platform

Your data is stored and processed on Google Firebase servers located in various regions of the world. Firebase uses modern encryption technologies and complies with international data security standards.

3.2. Data protection methods

  • Encryption in transit: all data is transmitted over a secure HTTPS connection
  • Encryption at rest: sensitive data is encrypted on the servers
  • Access control: only authorized staff and systems can access data
  • Regular security updates: we regularly update our security systems

3.3. Local caching

The app may save some data locally on your device to improve performance and offline operation. This data is kept in the device's secure storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

3.4. Data retention period

  • Profile data is kept until the account is deleted
  • Moments and events are kept until the user deletes them
  • Stories are deleted automatically after a set time
  • Chat messages are kept until the chat or account is deleted
  • Background location data is kept only for the active session

4. Sharing data with third parties

4.1. Service providers

We use the following services to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase: for data storage, user authentication and sending push notifications. Firebase is part of Google Cloud Platform and adheres to strict security standards.
  • Map service providers: to display maps and provide location features
  • Media hosting providers: to store and deliver photos and videos

These providers have access only to the data needed to provide their services and are obliged to protect your information in line with our standards.

4.2. Legal requirements

We may disclose your information to third parties in the following cases:

  • When required by law or at the request of government authorities
  • To protect our rights, property or safety
  • To protect the rights and safety of other users
  • In the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets (with notice to users)

4.3. Public content

Information you make public in the app (moments, events, profile) is available to other users of the app. Please be careful when posting personal information.

4.4. We do not sell data

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Your rights and control over your data

5.1. Right of access

You have the right to obtain information about what personal data we hold about you. You can view most of your data directly in the app in the profile settings section.

5.2. Right to rectification

You can change the information in your profile at any time via the app settings, including:

  • Nickname, profile description, interests
  • Profile photos
  • Map visibility settings
  • Notification settings

5.3. Right to erasure

You can delete:

  • Individual moments, events or messages
  • Your account entirely (via the app settings)

When you delete your account we delete all your personal data, except where we are required by law to keep certain information.

5.4. Right to withdraw consent

You can withdraw your consent to data processing at any time by deleting your account or turning off certain features in the app settings.

5.5. Control over location

You can:

  • Turn off location access in your device settings
  • Turn off map visibility in the app settings
  • Turn off background location tracking

Note that some app features may be unavailable without location access.

5.6. Right to data portability

You can request an export of your data in a machine-readable format by contacting us by email.

6. Location data and background tracking

6.1. Use of location

The app uses location data for the following features:

  • Showing your location on the map (optional)
  • Showing events and moments in your area
  • Attaching moments to specific places
  • The "Bump" feature — notifications when you meet friends
  • Personalizing content based on location

6.2. Background tracking

The app may track your location in the background for the "Bump" feature. This tracking happens only while the app is active or running in the background with your permission.

Important: background tracking may affect your device's battery usage. You can turn it off in the app or device settings at any time.

6.3. Control over location

You fully control the use of location:

  • The app asks for location permission on first launch
  • You can turn off access in iOS/Android settings at any time
  • You can turn off visibility on the map while keeping access to other features
  • Location data is not shared with third parties without your consent

7. Age restrictions and protection of minors

The YOLO app is intended for users over 13. If you are under 13, please do not use the app.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from users under 13. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

Parents and guardians should supervise minors' use of the app and may contact us to have a child's data deleted.

8. International data transfer

Your data may be stored and processed on servers located outside your country of residence. Firebase servers may be located in various regions of the world, including countries where data protection laws may differ from those of your country.

By providing us with your data, you consent to such transfer. We take all reasonable measures to keep your data secure regardless of its location.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

The app may use the device's local storage to:

  • Save app settings
  • Cache data to improve performance
  • Keep your sign-in state

This data is stored locally on your device and is not shared with third parties.

10. Data security

We take technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • Use of modern encryption protocols (HTTPS, TLS)
  • Regular updates of security systems
  • Limiting access to data to authorized staff only
  • Monitoring systems for suspicious activity
  • Regular data backups

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. Although we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to the privacy policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes:

  • Via a push notification in the app
  • By email (if provided)
  • By updating this page

By continuing to use the app after changes are made, you accept the updated policy. We recommend reviewing this page periodically.

12. Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app settings. When you delete your account:

  • All your personal data will be removed from our systems
  • Your moments, events and messages will be deleted
  • Your profile will become unavailable to other users
  • Some data may be kept in backups for up to 90 days

Note that information you made public (for example, moments other users replied to) may remain visible in the context of other users' replies.
